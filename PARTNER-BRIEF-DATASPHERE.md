## The customer situation the AE already knows

- **Classic SAP BW 7.5** — mainstream maintenance ends 31 December 2027, extended maintenance ends 31 December 2030 ([SAP · BW/4HANA End of Mainstream Maintenance Key Dates](https://www.sap.com/documents/2019/02/1ca4c940-3a7d-0010-87a3-c30de2ffd8ff.html); [SAP · NetWeaver 7.5 & BW/4HANA Maintenance Strategy](https://pages.community.sap.com/topics/abap/netweaver-maintenance-strategy)).
- **SAP BW/4HANA** — maintained through at least 2040. Not end-of-life, but innovation gravity has moved to Datasphere and SAP Analytics Cloud.
- **SAP Analysis for Microsoft Office (AfO)** — the Excel-on-BW front-end most customers actually live inside every day. Successor path: the SAC add-in for Microsoft 365.
- **Datasphere + SAC** — the sanctioned successor stack. A 30-day Datasphere free trial ([SAP · Experience SAP Datasphere](https://www.sap.com/products/data-cloud/datasphere/trial.html)) and a free SAC trial ([SAP · Analytics Cloud trial](https://www.sap.com/products/technology-platform/cloud-analytics/trial.html)) exist — but a trial on sample data is not the same thing as a working report against the customer's own P&L.

**The gap is not architecture. It is presales execution capacity in bounded units, fast enough to fit inside the trial window.**

## How the work actually gets done — the method

- **Perplexity projects** — each workstream lives in its own Perplexity project, with its own memory, sources, and threads.
- **Private repositories** — every artifact lands in a private repo with cryptographic hashes and clickable canonical links.
- **AI tools applied to SAP content** — schema reasoning, migration diffs, transport analysis, report parity checks, evidence packs. AI-authored, human-authorized. Speed from AI, accountability from the human sign-off canon around it.
- **Additive stance** — preserve what already works at the customer (their existing SI, their existing operating model, their existing BW where BW earns its keep) and introduce change only where it demonstrably improves the stated objective. The public [SAPfans.io](https://github.com/EVEglyphDesign/sapfans-io) commons is the same principle turned outward: preserve useful SAP knowledge, cite evidence, accept correction.

## Four packaged small work units

Each is quotable in a sentence, deliverable in weeks, fixed-price, and exit-friendly. If it doesn't go further, SCS hands back the repository and leaves.

### 1 · Datasphere Demo-Tenant Setup on the Customer's Own Data

Stand up a governed Datasphere trial tenant against the customer's own SAP source. Materialize a bounded subset of ACDOCA (or equivalent) and publish two or three analytic models finance recognizes on sight. Not sample data. **3–4 weeks, fixed price.**

### 2 · AfO-to-SAC Report Parity Sprint

The five AfO reports finance opens every morning, reproduced in the SAC add-in for Microsoft 365 against the customer's Datasphere semantic layer, with a side-by-side reconciliation to the cent. **4–6 weeks, fixed price per report bundle.**

### 3 · Governed Finance Proof — the Delivered Shape, Repointed

The reference pattern — Datasphere as the semantic hub, LangGraph over governed OData, Teams as the delivery surface — bounded to one finance lane the customer chooses (revenue recognition, receivables aging, or intercompany reconciliation). **6–8 weeks, fixed price, exit-friendly.**

### 4 · BW Replacement Proof

A bounded proof that Datasphere + SAC can perform the reporting the current BW installation performs today — validated by the customer's own finance team, exceptions explicitly listed. **6–10 weeks, fixed price** with named finance-user validation.

## Where SCS fits inside a full S/4 conversion

Full S/4HANA implementations belong with an SI. SCS has two roles when they come, both aligned to SAP license growth:

- **Flex labor supporting the incumbent SI** — named specialist capacity when the SI wants it. The SI retains the customer relationship and the implementation revenue.
- **An affordable alternative** — only if the customer has no incumbent and cannot fund a large SI, so demo momentum survives into the license close.

The deal the AE cares about is the SAP license. The Center of Excellence's role is to make sure the demo experience justifies that license, and to stay out of the way of the SI's implementation revenue.
