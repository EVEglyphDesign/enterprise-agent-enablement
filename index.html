A short note

The proof of concept worked. Let's help your people use it.

I'd like thirty minutes. Not to propose anything new — to talk about how we help the platform you already have find the hands it was built for.

What we set out to do, and what happened

The partnership deck we brought last December proposed an enterprise data mesh: a three-layer architecture spanning your SAP estate, Workday, Salesforce, and Bullhorn, with a semantic spine, a governed data fabric, and an AI access pattern delivered through Microsoft Teams. Phase 1 was strategy and architecture validation; Phase 2 was the foundation and lighthouse pilot on Contract-to-Cash.

We've done that work. Honestly, the proof of concept exceeded what we thought was possible when we scoped it. The historical reporting is all there. Delivering through Teams turned out to be straightforward. The AI agent answers cross-system questions safely, with the compliance controls the architecture promised. On our side we couldn't be happier with the result.

The problem we haven't solved yet

The gap isn't capability. The gap is that the people this platform was built for haven't had a real chance to use it yet. A working PoC is not the same thing as a tool your team reaches for on a Tuesday.

That last step is not another architecture question. It's a services question — who onboards which teams, in what order, with what kind of hand-holding, and what tells you it's working. That is where I'd like to help.

What I'd like to talk about

Thirty minutes. You tell me who inside your organization would benefit most if they started using the mesh next quarter, and what has kept them from it so far. I tell you honestly what an adoption motion looks like against that specific list — which team goes first, what "in their daily rhythm" actually means, and how we know six weeks in whether it's working.

Nothing to prepare. Nothing to review. Just a call.

Who's writing

I spent most of my career building a services company — scaled to about three hundred people, with a strong reputation across a wide range of clients. I sold it. Now I'm building the next one, deliberately, with the specialists who built the platform you already have. My job on this call is not to sell you anything. It is to help finish delivering what we started together.
