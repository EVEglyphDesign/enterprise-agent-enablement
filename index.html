An Adoption Fitness Check

The shape I'd propose for the first six weeks is a short, structured read of where adoption actually stands. Four dimensions, one page each, one prioritized roadmap out the other side.

  1. Awareness. Do the named business users on the target teams know the mesh exists, what it can answer, and how to reach it from Teams? Awareness is the cheapest gap to close and often the one that has been assumed away.
  2. Confidence. When a user gets an answer from the agent, do they trust it enough to act on it — or do they still re-run the report in the old tool to check? Confidence is earned once, from a specific team, on a specific question.
  3. Fit. Does the platform answer the questions those specific teams actually ask on a Tuesday, or does it answer a slightly different set the pilot optimized for? Fit is a listening exercise before it is a build one.
  4. Habit. Six weeks in, are the same users coming back on their own, without a nudge? Are they bringing colleagues? Habit is the only dimension the platform team cannot cause from the outside.

The output is a prioritized roadmap — which team goes first, what hand-holding they need, what tells you six weeks in that it is working. Not a re-architecture. Not a rebrand of the platform. The small, disciplined services motion that turns a delivered capability into a used one.
